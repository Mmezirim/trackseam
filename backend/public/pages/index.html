Trackseam

Client measurement records for tailors and fashion designers

Trackseam lets tailors and fashion houses register clients, record measurements for every outfit, and export clean PDF measurement sheets from Android, iPhone or the web.

  • A private registry of your clients, each with a short client ID
  • Preset measurement sheets for shirts, trousers, dresses, suits, skirts, agbada and kaftan, or your own custom fields
  • Search clients by name and export any measurement to PDF
